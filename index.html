FAQ

Common questions.

Is studY free to use?
Yes — joining and browsing groups is completely free for all Yonsei IID students.
Do I need a Yonsei email?
Yes — a @yonsei.ac.kr address keeps groups within the academic community. We verify once at sign-up.
How does schedule matching work?
We compare your selected time cells with all students in your chosen courses, scoring groups by overlap quality and shared goals. Your match percentage reflects actual availability fit.
Can I be in more than one group?
Absolutely. Most IID students join 2–3 groups across different courses. You manage all of them from My Page.
What if my course isn't in the list?
Use the "Other course not listed?" text field in Step 3. Type any course name and press Enter to add it as a tag.
Where do groups typically meet on campus?
Veritas B Hall seminar rooms, Underwood Library 2F, Café Underwood, and the IID studio in Veritas A are popular spots. Virtual groups share Zoom links in the group chatroom.
